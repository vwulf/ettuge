# Rasaphala (ರಸಫಲ)
**Author:** Gorur Ramaswamy Iyengar



---

## Summary

*Rasaphala* is listed among the works of Gorur Ramaswamy Iyengar, a Kannada writer from Gorur in Hassan district who was also active in the Gandhian freedom movement. Gorur is best known for humorous essays and sketches of rural life such as *Halliya Chitragalu* and *Namma Oorina Rasikaru*, for novels such as *Hemavathi* and *Urvashi*, and for the travelogue *Amerikadalli Goruru*, which won the Sahitya Akademi Award.

Little published criticism exists on *Rasaphala* specifically, and detailed descriptions of its contents are not readily available in English sources. It is generally discussed, if at all, as part of Gorur's broader prose output, which is characterised by warm humour, close observation of village characters and a conversational style.

---

## Critical Takeaways

- **Author's reputation:** Gorur is regarded as one of the leading Kannada humorists, known for gentle satire and affectionate portraits of rural Karnataka.
- **Rural sensibility:** Critics of his work emphasise his depiction of village life in the Hemavathi river region and its characters.
- **Gandhian context:** His writing is often read in the light of his participation in the freedom movement and Gandhian values.
- **Limited coverage:** Little published criticism of *Rasaphala* itself exists; available discussion concerns Gorur's work as a whole.

# Lords of the Deccan: Southern India from the Chalukyas to the Cholas
**Author:** Anirudh Kanisetti | **Published:** 2022



---

## Summary

*Lords of the Deccan*, published by Juggernaut in 2022, tells the political and cultural history of the Deccan plateau and the far south during the early medieval period. It follows the rise of the Chalukyas of Badami, their rivalry with the Pallavas of Kanchi, the dramatic expansion of the Rashtrakutas, the later Chalukyas of Kalyani, and the maritime and military ambitions of the Cholas.

Kanisetti, a young historian and writer who also hosts history podcasts, weaves political events together with temple-building, religion, courtly culture, trade networks and the role of land grants and inscriptions. He argues that these dynasties were sophisticated, competitive states whose achievements have been underappreciated in narratives of Indian history centred on the north.

The book is written for a general audience, with vivid scene-setting and an accessible style, while drawing on epigraphic sources and academic historians.

---

## Critical Takeaways

- **Restoring the south:** Reviewers praise it for bringing the Deccan's medieval empires into popular history, where they had often been neglected.
- **Readable synthesis:** It is widely described as engaging and well paced, accessible to readers without background in the period.
- **Use of scholarship:** Critics note its reliance on epigraphy and existing academic work; some academic readers describe it as a synthesis rather than original research.
- **Interpretive framing:** Some reviewers found its modern analogies and vivid characterisations of kings occasionally speculative given the sparse sources.
- **Debut reception:** It was well received as a first book, establishing Kanisetti as a prominent popular historian of medieval India.

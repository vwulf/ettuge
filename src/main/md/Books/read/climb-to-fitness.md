# Climb to Fitness: The Ultimate Guide to Customizing a Powerful Workout on the Wall
**Author:** Julie Ellison | **Published:** 2018



---

## Summary

*Climb to Fitness* by Julie Ellison, a climber and former editor at *Climbing* magazine, presents climbing as a fitness program in its own right. It is aimed largely at the growing population of gym climbers, and shows how to structure sessions on bouldering and rope walls to build strength, power, endurance and technique.

The book describes climbing-specific drills alongside off-the-wall exercises for core strength, antagonist muscles, mobility and injury prevention. Workouts are organized so readers can customize them by goal and ability level, and each exercise is illustrated with photographs.

It is a practical training guide rather than a narrative climbing book, intended to help recreational climbers train more systematically.

---

## Critical Takeaways

- **Limited critical coverage:** As a niche training guide, it has drawn mainly reader reviews rather than formal criticism.
- **Practical structure:** Readers note its ready-to-use workouts and photographs as useful for gym climbers wanting more structure.
- **Accessible level:** It is generally seen as suited to beginner and intermediate climbers more than advanced athletes seeking specialized periodization.
- **Balanced fitness:** Its attention to antagonist training and injury prevention is seen as a strength in a sport prone to finger and shoulder injuries.

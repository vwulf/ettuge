## Summary

*There and Back* (Ten Speed Press, 2021) collects Chin's photographs from expeditions around the world, including climbs and ski descents in the Himalaya and Karakoram, the first ascent of the Shark's Fin on Meru in the Garhwal Himalaya, and big-wall climbing in Yosemite with partners such as Conrad Anker and Alex Honnold. The images range from athletes in extreme positions to landscapes and expedition life.

Chin accompanies the photographs with short essays and anecdotes about the circumstances behind them, the risks involved, the friends he climbed with and some he lost, and his path from a climbing bum to a professional photographer and filmmaker. The book thus doubles as an informal memoir of a life in the mountains.

---

## Critical Takeaways

- **Visual achievement:** Reviews and outdoor media praise the photography as among the most striking in adventure imagery, noting Chin's ability to shoot while climbing at a high level himself.
- **Stories behind the images:** The accompanying texts are valued for showing the practical and emotional reality of expedition work.
- **Coffee-table format:** It is largely regarded as a showcase book, more of a visual retrospective than a narrative memoir.
- **Context:** Chin's reputation from *Meru* (2015) and *Free Solo* (2018, Academy Award for Best Documentary Feature, co-directed with Elizabeth Chai Vasarhelyi) shaped its reception.
- **Limited critical coverage:** As a photo book it received coverage mainly in outdoor and photography outlets rather than literary review.

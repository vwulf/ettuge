# Take the Lead: Hanging On, Letting Go, and Conquering Life's Hardest Climbs
**Author:** Sasha DiGiulian | **Published:** 2023



---

## Summary

*Take the Lead* (St. Martin's Press, 2023) is DiGiulian's first book. She describes growing up in the Washington, D.C. area, finding climbing as a child at a gym, rising through youth competitions, and winning the overall women's title at the 2011 World Championships. She then recounts her move into outdoor and big-wall climbing, including first female ascents of hard routes, while balancing professional sponsorship and a degree at Columbia University.

The memoir is also about the pressures behind a public, sponsored athletic career: body image in a sport obsessed with weight, serious injury and surgery, online harassment, the sexism she encountered, and grief after her father's death. She frames climbing as a source of resilience and argues for greater equity and representation in the outdoor world.

---

## Critical Takeaways

- **Reception:** Publishers Weekly gave it a positive review, calling it gripping and uplifting.
- **Candour:** Reviewers note her openness about injury, grief and the pressures on women athletes as the book's strongest element.
- **For climbers and non-climbers:** It is generally described as accessible to general readers, with climbing detail kept understandable.
- **Inspirational framing:** Some readers find the motivational tone and brand-conscious voice of a sponsored athlete less compelling than the more searching climbing memoirs in the genre.
- **Limited critical coverage:** Beyond trade reviews and outdoor media, the book has received modest literary criticism.

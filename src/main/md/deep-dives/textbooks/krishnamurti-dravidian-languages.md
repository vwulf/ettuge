This PDF is a reflowed text copy, so its page numbers do not match the printed book; the PDF page ranges here are for this file. Chapter introductions and conclusions are not broken out, and the bibliography and indexes are not covered. Summaries are in our own words; the text stays in the PDF.

## The book in one paragraph

The standard one-volume survey of the Dravidian family by its leading comparativist. Krishnamurti introduces the name, prehistory and distribution of some 26 languages and their contact with Indo-Aryan, then gives descriptive phonology and the history of the Telugu–Kannada and Tamil–Malayalam scripts. The core is comparative reconstruction: Proto-Dravidian phonemes, phonotactics and sound changes; roots, formatives and derivation; nominal morphology (gender–number, cases, pronouns, numerals); the verb (stems, transitivity and causatives, tense, personal endings, finite and non-finite forms, negation, serial and compound verbs); adjectives, adverbs and clitics; syntax; and the lexicon including loanwords and expressives. It closes with his subgrouping of the family into South I, South II, Central and North Dravidian and the antiquity of Proto-Dravidian.

# Textbooks

Books from the Drive *Books* folder, each broken down from the top: the book in a paragraph, then each chapter, then each section, and finally the pages in the PDF where that section sits. The summaries are in our own words; the books themselves stay in Drive, so the page links open only for the owner.

Partial PDFs (samples, excerpts, unfinished drafts) are marked as such on their pages. Scanned books that need OCR come later.

## AI and Machine Learning

- [Concise Machine Learning](shewchuk-concise-machine-learning) (Shewchuk): Berkeley CS 189: SVMs to spectral clustering
- [Deep Learning and Computational Physics](deep-learning-computational-physics) (Ray, Pinti, Oberai): PINNs, DeepONet, FNO, neural ODEs (lecture notes)
- [Foundations of Reinforcement Learning with Applications in Finance](rao-jelvis-rl-in-finance) (Ashwin Rao and Tikhon Jelvis): MDPs, DP, approximate DP and RL algorithms with Python, applied to asset allocation, derivatives and trading.
- [High-Dimensional Probability](high-dimensional-probability) (Roman Vershynin): Concentration, random matrices, chaining and sparse recovery, each with a data-science application.
- [Mathematical Theory of the Transformer, Vol. I](cirrincione-mathematical-theory-of-the-transformer) (Giansalvo Cirrincione): Attention via the bilinear score matrix: symmetric/antisymmetric split, head taxonomies, training dynamics, INCRT growth.
- [Multimodal Deep Learning](multimodal-deep-learning) (LMU seminar): image–text models: CLIP, DALL-E, diffusion (2023)
- [Optimization for Machine Learning](gartner-jaggi-optimization-ml) (Gärtner, Jaggi): GD, SGD, proximal, Newton, BFGS, Frank-Wolfe with rates
- [Prompt Engineering](prompt-engineering) (Boonstra (Google)): sampling settings, prompting techniques, best practices
- [Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction (2nd ed.)](sutton-barto-reinforcement-learning) (Richard Sutton and Andrew Barto): Bandits, MDPs, DP, Monte Carlo, TD, n-step, planning, function approximation, off-policy, traces, policy gradients, psychology, neuroscience.
- [Understanding Deep Learning](understanding-deep-learning) (Prince): shallow networks to diffusion models, 18 chapters

## Computer Science

- [Algebraic Effects for Functional Programming (Type Directed Compilation of Row-Typed Algebraic Effects)](leijen-type-directed-compilation-row-typed-effects) (Daan Leijen): Koka's handlers end to end: row-typed inference, semantics, soundness, and a type-directed selective CPS compilation.
- [An Introduction to Functional Programming Through Lambda Calculus](michaelson-lambda-calculus) (Michaelson): encodings, evaluation, SML and LISP
- [Calculating Compilers](meijer-calculating-compilers) (Meijer): compilers derived from semantics; bananas and lenses
- [Compilers: Course Notes](kiselyov-compilers-notes) (Kiselyov): an OCaml compiler to x86-64 in tagless-final style (first 28 pages)
- [Computability, Unsolvability, Randomness](simpson-computability-randomness) (Simpson): recursion theory to Martin-Löf randomness
- [Distributed Computing Through Combinatorial Topology](distributed-computing-combinatorial-topology) (Herlihy, Kozlov and Rajsbaum): Solvability of distributed tasks as a question about simplicial complexes and their maps.
- [Effect Oriented Programming](effect-oriented-programming) (Frasure, Eckel, Ward): ZIO effects, layers, typed errors, resilience
- [Foundations of Probabilistic Programming](foundations-of-probabilistic-programming) (Barthe, Katoen and Silva (eds.)): Semantics and verification of probabilistic programs: measures, couplings, runtimes, martingales, Bayesian logic, QIF.
- [Functional Programming Strategies in Scala with Cats (draft)](welsh-functional-programming-strategies-scala-with-cats) (Noel Welsh): ADTs, codata, type classes, interpreters, the Cats type classes, and case studies, in Scala 3.
- [Functional Programming in Lean](functional-programming-in-lean) (David Thrane Christiansen): Lean 4 as a programming language: types, IO, type classes, monads, transformers, dependent types, induction proofs, verified fast code.
- [Handling Algebraic Effects](plotkin-pretnar-handling-algebraic-effects) (Gordon D. Plotkin & Matija Pretnar): Effect handlers as homomorphisms out of free models: the calculus, examples, semantics and handler correctness.
- [Haskell: The Craft of Functional Programming (3rd ed.)](thompson-haskell-craft-of-functional-programming) (Simon Thompson): Haskell from basics to type classes, ADTs, laziness, monads, DSLs and performance, with case studies.
- [Implementing Functional Languages: a tutorial](implementing-functional-languages) (Simon Peyton Jones and David Lester): Building lazy evaluators step by step: template instantiation, G-machine, TIM, parallel G-machine, lambda lifting.
- [Koka: Programming with Row-polymorphic Effect Types](leijen-koka-row-polymorphic-effects) (Daan Leijen): Inferred effect rows with duplicate labels: Koka's type system, semantics and soundness of exn, div and st.
- [Kyo: a book-style guide to the documentation](kyo-book) (Kyo maintainers (getkyo.io)): The getkyo.io module docs arranged as a book: A < S, prelude and core effects, kernel, styles, concurrency, schema, HTTP, AI, interop.
- [Lambda-Calculus and Combinators](hindley-seldin-lambda-calculus-combinators) (J. Roger Hindley and Jonathan Seldin): λ-calculus and CL side by side: reduction, computability, typing, pure type systems and models.
- [Learn Programming with OCaml](conchon-filliatre-learn-programming-with-ocaml) (Sylvain Conchon and Jean-Christophe Filliâtre): OCaml through small programs, then classic data structures and algorithms implemented functionally.
- [Mathematics and Computation](wigderson-mathematics-and-computation) (Avi Wigderson): Panoramic survey of complexity theory, randomness, proofs and its links to mathematics and science.
- [Modern Systems Programming with Scala Native](scala-native-systems-programming) (Whaling): pointers, processes, libuv, LMDB
- [Perhaps Not The Answer You Were Expecting](mcbride-perhaps-not-the-answer) (McBride): Stack Overflow answers: applicatives, derivatives, dependent types
- [Practical Foundations for Programming Languages (2nd ed., abbreviated)](harper-practical-foundations-for-programming-languages) (Robert Harper): Statics and dynamics for every language feature: T, PCF, System F, state, control, parallelism, concurrency, modules.
- [Pragmatic Type-Level Design](granin-pragmatic-type-level-design) (Alexander Granin): Type-level programming for application design in Haskell, with Rust and Scala 3 counterparts.
- [Principles of Eventual Consistency](burckhardt-eventual-consistency) (Burckhardt): histories, abstract executions, CAP, protocols
- [Programming Well: Abstraction and Design in Computation](shieber-programming-well) (Stuart M. Shieber): CS51 in OCaml: types, higher-order functions, modules, semantics models, laziness, objects, MiniML.
- [Programming in Scala (5th ed., Scala 3)](odersky-programming-in-scala-5e) (Odersky, Spoon, Venners and Sommers): The standard Scala 3 book, from basics through traits, patterns, collections, types, givens and typeclasses.
- [Structured Asynchrony with Algebraic Effects](leijen-structured-asynchrony-algebraic-effects) (Daan Leijen): async/await, interleaving, cancellation, promises and ambient state as a Koka library of effect handlers.
- [The Formal Semantics of Programming Languages](winskel-formal-semantics) (Glynn Winskel): Operational, denotational and axiomatic semantics; domain theory, recursive types and concurrency.
- [The Little Book of Semaphores](little-book-of-semaphores) (Allen B. Downey): Concurrency puzzles solved with semaphores, from classic problems to original ones.
- [The Science of Functional Programming](winitzki-science-of-functional-programming) (Sergei Winitzki): FP derived by symbolic reasoning in Scala: Curry-Howard, functors, monads, applicatives, free constructions, transformers.
- [Theorem Proving in Lean 4](theorem-proving-in-lean-4) (Avigad, de Moura, Kong, Ullrich): dependent types, tactics, inductive types, type classes
- [Type Theory and Functional Programming](thompson-type-theory-functional-programming) (Simon Thompson): Martin-Löf type theory as logic and programming language, with program development and extensions.
- [Unix System Programming in OCaml](unix-system-programming-ocaml) (Leroy, Rémy): files, processes, signals, pipes, sockets, threads
- [Zionomicon](zionomicon) (John A. De Goes, Adam Fraser and Milad Khajavi): ZIO 2 in depth: fibers, concurrency structures, resources, layers, STM, schedules, streams and ZIO Test.

## Mathematics

- [A Synopsis of Elementary Results in Pure Mathematics](carr-synopsis-of-pure-mathematics) (G. S. Carr): The 1886 compendium Ramanujan learned from: thousands of results in algebra, calculus, geometry, with journal index.
- [An Infinite Descent into Pure Mathematics](newstead-infinite-descent) (Clive Newstead): Intro to proof: logic, sets, functions, induction, relations, then number theory, combinatorics, reals, probability.
- [Basic Category Theory for Computer Scientists](pierce-basic-category-theory) (Pierce): limits, adjoints, CCCs, domain equations
- [Book of Proof](hammack-book-of-proof) (Richard Hammack): Transition-to-proofs text: sets, logic, proof techniques, induction, relations, functions, cardinality.
- [Category Theory for Programmers (Scala edition)](milewski-category-theory-for-programmers) (Bartosz Milewski): Category theory through code: functors, Yoneda, adjunctions, monads, comonads, F-algebras, ends, Kan extensions.
- [Category Theory for Scientists](spivak-category-theory-for-scientists) (Spivak): ologs, databases, limits, adjoints, monads, operads
- [Conceptual Mathematics](lawvere-conceptual-mathematics) (F. William Lawvere and Stephen Schanuel): A first course in categories from sets and maps to products, exponentials and toposes.
- [Discrete Mathematics and Its Applications (8th ed.)](rosen-discrete-mathematics) (Kenneth H. Rosen): Logic, proofs, sets, algorithms, number theory, counting, probability, recurrences, graphs, trees, automata.
- [Everything You Always Wanted to Know About Mathematics](sullivan-everything-you-wanted-to-know-about-mathematics) (Brendan W. Sullivan): Conversational intro to proof: induction, sets, logic, relations, functions, cardinality, combinatorics.
- [Geometric Algebra Applications, Vol. I](bayro-corrochano-geometric-algebra-applications-1) (Eduardo Bayro-Corrochano): Clifford algebra for vision, graphics and neurocomputing: calculus, Lie groups, conformal GA, transforms, applications.
- [Geometric Structures on Manifolds](goldman-geometric-structures-on-manifolds) (William M. Goldman): (G,X)-structures: affine and projective geometry, developing maps, completeness, convex projective surfaces.
- [Graph Theory and Additive Combinatorics](zhao-graph-theory-additive-combinatorics) (Yufei Zhao): Extremal graphs, regularity, pseudorandomness, graph limits, Roth, Freiman and Green–Tao.
- [Homotopy Type Theory: Univalent Foundations of Mathematics](hott-book) (Univalent Foundations Program (IAS)): Type theory as homotopy: univalence, HITs, n-types, synthetic homotopy theory, categories, reals.
- [Introducing Category Theory (2nd ed.)](smith-introducing-category-theory) (Peter Smith): Slow, careful intro: limits, exponentials, functors, Yoneda, adjunctions, elementary toposes.
- [Introduction to Logic and to the Methodology of Deductive Sciences](tarski-introduction-to-logic) (Tarski): sentential calculus, classes, relations, axiomatics
- [Intuitionistic Type Theory](martin-lof-intuitionistic-type-theory) (Martin-Löf): meaning explanations and the type formers
- [Intuitive Topology](prasolov-intuitive-topology) (Prasolov): knots, homeomorphisms, vector fields, surfaces
- [Linear Algebra Done Wrong](linear-algebra-done-wrong) (Sergei Treil): Rigorous first course with early determinants, through SVD, quadratic forms, tensors and Jordan form.
- [Linear Algebra for Computer Vision, Robotics, and Machine Learning](gallier-linear-algebra-vision-robotics-ml) (Jean Gallier and Jocelyn Quaintance): Rigorous, computational linear algebra: factorizations, norms, spectral theory, quaternions, Laplacians, SVD.
- [Linear Algebra via Exterior Products](linear-algebra-via-exterior-products) (Sergei Winitzki): Coordinate-free linear algebra where determinants, trace and Jordan form come from wedge products.
- [Linear Algebra, Theory and Applications](kuttler-linear-algebra) (Kenneth Kuttler): Matrix computation and theory: factorizations, canonical forms, spectral theory, SVD, norms, numerics.
- [Mathematics in Lean](mathematics-in-lean) (Avigad, Massot): Formalizing mathematics in Lean 4 with Mathlib: calculation, logic, sets, number theory, combinatorics, structures and hierarchies, algebra, linear algebra, topology, calculus, measure theory.
- [Module Theory: An Approach to Linear Algebra](blyth-module-theory) (T. S. Blyth): Linear algebra over rings: exact sequences, projective and injective modules, tensors, PIDs and canonical forms.
- [Notes on Alpöge's Proof of the Riemann Hypothesis](alpoge-riemann-hypothesis-notes) (AI-generated (Claude), printed by Taylor Dupuy): Lecture notes on the Lean-only RH/GRH proof: Stepanov's method as the model, the proof from twenty black boxes, then the tools; unreviewed.
- [Surreal Numbers (opening chapters)](surreal-numbers) (Knuth): Conway’s numbers rediscovered as a story
- [The Discrete Algebra of the Fourier Transform](discrete-algebra-fourier-transform) (Gabriel Peyré): Fourier analysis on finite groups, the DFT and FFT, finite-field transforms and group representations.
- [The Riddle of Scheherazade](smullyan-riddle-of-scheherazade) (Raymond M. Smullyan): Logic and recreational puzzles: liars and truth-tellers, coercive logic, Gödel-flavored puzzles, paradoxes.

## Linguistics and Languages

- [A Manual of Modern Kannada](zydenbos-manual-of-modern-kannada) (Robert J. Zydenbos): Grammar course in modern written Kannada: 19 lessons from nominal sentences to moods, plus sandhi, numerals and script.
- [The Dravidian Languages](krishnamurti-dravidian-languages) (Bhadriraju Krishnamurti): Comparative survey: Proto-Dravidian phonology, morphology, verb, syntax, lexicon and subgrouping.
- [The Unicode Standard 18.0: Core Specification](unicode-standard-core-spec) (The Unicode Consortium): Architecture, conformance, properties and script-by-script encoding, including Kannada and other Indic scripts.

## Physics and Engineering

- [Electricity and Magnetism (2nd ed.)](bleaney-electricity-and-magnetism) (B. I. Bleaney and B. Bleaney): Classic Oxford text: fields, circuits, waves, waveguides, tubes, noise, dielectrics, metals, semiconductors, magnetism, NMR.
- [Something Deeply Hidden](carroll-something-deeply-hidden) (Sean Carroll): Quantum mechanics from the wave function up: entanglement, Bell, decoherence and Many-Worlds, the Born rule, rival interpretations, and emergent spacetime from entanglement.

> T. S. Blyth · 2nd edition 1990, electronic edition 2018 (CC BY 4.0) · [the PDF in Drive](https://drive.google.com/file/d/12dZQ039nTi2mc65o8wk0jKXEgwdTE0fj/view)

PDF page = printed page + 5. The book has no numbered sections, so each chapter is one leaf. Summaries are in our own words; the text stays in the PDF.

## The book in one paragraph

An algebra-first treatment of linear algebra that works with modules over rings and specializes to vector spaces when the ring is a field. Blyth covers submodules, morphisms and exact sequences, isomorphism theorems and chain conditions, products and coproducts, free and projective modules, duality, matrices, inner product spaces, injective and semisimple modules, the Jacobson radical, tensor products and flatness, tensor and exterior algebras with determinants, the structure of finitely generated modules over a PID, and finally canonical forms (rational, Jordan) and diagonalization of normal transformations.

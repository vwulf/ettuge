## The book in one paragraph

A classic Oxford undergraduate and early-graduate text covering electricity and magnetism from first principles to the physics of materials, in rationalized m.k.s. units. It treats electrostatics and potential theory, steady currents and conduction, magnetostatics, induction and circuits, measurement instruments, magnetic materials, AC theory, Maxwell’s equations and electromagnetic waves, filters, transmission lines and waveguides, vacuum-tube electronics up to microwave devices, and noise; then the microscopic theory of dielectrics, electrons in metals and semiconductors, paramagnetism, ferromagnetism and antiferromagnetism, and magnetic resonance, ending with systems of units.

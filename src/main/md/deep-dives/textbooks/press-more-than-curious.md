# More Than Curious: A Science Memoir

> William H. Press · Darwin-Finch Publishing, 2023 (Creative Commons) · [the PDF in Drive](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1uk2E1vOOiOb5uuWrx1inygjBGpK9f2_i/view)

A memoir, not a textbook: its 70 short chapters are grouped here into eight periods of the author’s life, which are our grouping, not the book’s. Summaries stick to the science, institutions and public events described. Summaries are in our own words; the text stays in the PDF.

## The book in one paragraph

The memoir of astrophysicist and computational scientist William Press, co-author of Numerical Recipes, told through people and institutions he saw up close. It runs from his family’s émigré roots and a Caltech-faculty childhood in Altadena, through Harvard, weapons-lab summers at Livermore with Edward Teller, and graduate work at Caltech with Kip Thorne on black holes and gravitational waves alongside Feynman and Chandrasekhar; then Princeton, a long Harvard career as astronomy chair and in JASON defense advising, the making of Numerical Recipes, the shift of science to computing, deputy directorship of Los Alamos during the Wen Ho Lee affair and the Cerro Grande fire, arms-control dialogues, and service on President Obama’s PCAST, ending with the COVID era.

## The book in one paragraph

The most widely used discrete mathematics textbook for computer science students. It covers logic and proof methods, sets, functions, sequences and matrices, algorithms and their complexity, number theory and cryptography, induction and recursion, counting, discrete probability, recurrences and generating functions, relations, graphs and trees, Boolean algebra and circuits, and models of computation from grammars and finite-state machines to Turing machines.

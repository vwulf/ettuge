A collection catalogue of the South Sumatra State Museum in Palembang, written in Indonesian; the summaries here are in English. Printed page = PDF page − 8.

## The book in one paragraph

A catalogue of the South Sumatra State Museum, organized by exhibition room. After the museum’s history, mission and profile, it walks through roughly seventy objects, each with photographs and a short description. Room I covers prehistory (elephant and shell fossils, burial jars, a cave skeleton, a dugout boat), Chinese trade ceramics from the Tang to the Qing, and finds from the Musi river. Room II holds the core of the collection for historians: the Srivijaya-era Old Malay inscriptions (Kedukan Bukit, Talang Tuo, Telaga Batu, Kota Kapur, Palas Pasemah, Karang Berahi), alongside colonial and Japanese-occupation objects and Hindu-Buddhist stone sculpture. Room III shows Palembang material culture: carved wood, household and fishing implements, batik, jumputan and songket textiles, and wedding costumes.

## The book in one paragraph

A how-to book by the rider who did most to create off-road unicycling, covering its two disciplines: muni (mountain unicycling on trails) and trials (hopping and balancing over obstacles). After the history of the sport and a detailed guide to equipment, it covers safety and trail ethics, then teaches trials skills in progression (still stands, balance lines, hops, gaps, pedal grabs, drops) and the trail techniques for climbing, descending, braking, technical terrain, bad conditions and geared hubs. It is heavily photographic, with each skill broken into steps and common mistakes.

> William M. Goldman · AMS Graduate Studies in Mathematics 227, 2022 (author’s version, December 2021) · [the PDF in Drive](https://drive.google.com/file/d/10yFnWsD1P7XlTAoeDRTkzP-Ag6PGWq8c/view)

Exercise and notes subsections are not broken out; only the first level of numbered sections is listed. Appendices are not broken down. Summaries are in our own words; the text stays in the PDF.

## The book in one paragraph

A graduate text on locally homogeneous geometric structures in the sense of Ehresmann and Thurston: manifolds modeled on a homogeneous space X with transition maps in a Lie group G. Part One develops the model geometries themselves (affine and projective geometry, duality and non-Euclidean geometry as subgeometries, convexity and the Hilbert metric). Part Two sets up (G,X)-structures in general: developing maps and holonomy, examples (Hopf and radiant manifolds), deformation spaces and representation varieties, and completeness. Part Three focuses on affine and projective structures: Euler-characteristic obstructions, affine structures on Lie groups and left-symmetric algebras, parallel volume and Markus’s conjecture, hyperbolicity, convex projective and complex-projective structures on surfaces, and geometric structures on 3-manifolds including Margulis spacetimes.

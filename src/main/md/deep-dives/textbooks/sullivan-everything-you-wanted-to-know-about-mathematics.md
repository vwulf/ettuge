## The book in one paragraph

A conversational, guided introduction to abstract mathematics and proof-writing written for CMU’s first-year course. Part I teaches how to think mathematically: what mathematics and proof are, induction informally and then rigorously, sets, and logic with quantifiers and negation. Part II applies this to core topics: relations, orders and equivalence relations with modular arithmetic, functions and cardinality, and combinatorics (counting principles, double counting, selections with repetition, pigeonhole and inclusion–exclusion).

1. [What Is Mathematics?](ch1): What mathematics and proof are.

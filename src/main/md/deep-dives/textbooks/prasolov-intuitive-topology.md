# Intuitive Topology

> V. V. Prasolov · AMS Mathematical World 4 (1995); Universities Press India reprint · [the PDF in Drive](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1-qEba00qJYYa8UQ4xZhwlSQ0HW2FRx6j/view)

Short chapters without numbered sections, so each chapter is a leaf here. Summaries are in our own words; the text stays in the PDF.

## The book in one paragraph

A short, picture-driven introduction to topology for readers without the formal machinery. It starts with deforming elastic objects and with knots, uses spanning surfaces and a simple diagram invariant to show that the trefoil cannot be untied, and then makes “same shape” precise through homeomorphisms. The second half turns to vector fields: the index of a singular point, the Brouwer fixed-point theorem, why every vector field on a sphere vanishes somewhere, and fixed-point-free and periodic maps. It ends with a tour of two-dimensional surfaces and their classification. Intuition comes first, with proofs sketched where they stay elementary.

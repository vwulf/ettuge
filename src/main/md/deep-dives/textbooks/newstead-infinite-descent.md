## The book in one paragraph

An introduction to proof and pure mathematics for first-year undergraduates that teaches logical reasoning, proof-writing and problem-solving together. Part I covers the core: propositional logic and quantifiers, sets, functions, induction from Peano’s axioms, relations and equivalence relations, and finite and countable sets. Part II tours topics: elementary number theory and modular arithmetic, enumerative combinatorics, real numbers with inequalities, completeness and series, cardinal arithmetic, discrete probability, and orders, lattices and inductively defined sets. Appendices give proof-writing advice and foundational constructions of the number systems.

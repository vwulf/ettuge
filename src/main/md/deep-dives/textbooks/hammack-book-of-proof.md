> Richard Hammack · 3rd edition, 2018 (free online) · [the PDF in Drive](https://drive.google.com/file/d/126YJ4snIabrXmXGNyASX9oREaHLiJKyu/view)

## The book in one paragraph

A transition-to-proofs textbook that teaches how to read and write mathematical proofs. It starts with sets, logic and counting, then covers the standard techniques for conditional statements (direct, contrapositive, contradiction), other kinds of statements (if-and-only-if, existence, uniqueness), proofs about sets, disproof by counterexample and mathematical induction, and finally relations, functions, a chapter of ε–δ proofs in calculus, and cardinality of sets including Cantor’s theorem.

**Contents**

1. [I Fundamentals](ch1): The language of sets, logic and counting.

3. [III More on Proof](ch3): Proving other statements and disproving.
4. [IV Relations, Functions and Cardinality](ch4): Relations, functions and the sizes of sets.

## The book in one paragraph

A grammar-based course in standard modern written Kannada for English-speaking learners, by a long-time scholar of Kannada literature. A substantial preface situates the language (its linguistic, literary, historical and social significance, oldest literature, dialects, script, Dravidian family and relation to Sanskrit). Nineteen lessons then build the grammar step by step, from nominal sentences, gender and cases through verb classes, tenses and negation, participles, verbal nouns and the infinitive, quotatives, moods, conditionals and auxiliaries, each with Kannada script, transliteration and exercises. Reference chapters cover sandhi, numerals, irregular verbs, colloquial forms and the sound system and script. It follows the traditional, Sanskrit-influenced grammatical vocabulary in places, unlike DNS Bhat’s native-term approach.

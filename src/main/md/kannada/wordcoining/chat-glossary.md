# Glossary: native words found or coined in one long chat

*Every word from the Rudram, physics/grammar and everyday sessions, with its rationale and how well it is supported. The detailed discussions stay on [Śrī Rudram & Ritual](rudram-ritual), [Physics & Grammar](physics-grammar) and [Everyday Words](everyday).*

## How to read the status column

| Tag | Meaning |
|-----|---------|
| **Bhat** | Found in Bhat's own books. Entries marked B31 were checked against the Book 31 dictionary text in this repo; B15 against Book 15. |
| **Existing** | An ordinary Kannada word that looks native, but I did not find it in Bhat. Treat its origin as unchecked. |
| **Coined** | A session coinage built from native roots with Bhat's suffixes. Not attested anywhere. |
| **Unverified** | Claim or etymology I could not confirm. Needs a check before anyone relies on it. |

**Precedence rule:** a Bhat-attested word beats a session coinage. Where both exist below, the Bhat word is the default and the coinage is an alternative.

**Corrections made along the way:** ಕಾಲ, ಬಲ, ಮನ and ಚಲನ are Sanskrit and were replaced. The elision ತನ್ನ + ಕೆಲಸ → ತನ್ನೆಲಸ had no basis and was withdrawn.

# Karate & Kobudō — ಬರಿಗೈಕಾಳಗ

*Native Kannada names for Okinawan karate and kobudō terms: weapons, stances, techniques and kata.*

A handful of native roots does most of the work: ಕಾಳಗ (fight), ಕೋಲ್ (stick), ಇರ್ಪು (stance, used for Japanese *dachi*), ಗುದ್ದು (punch), ಒದೆ (kick), ತಡೆ (block). Weapons are named by their shape, and stances by what the body does.

---

## Arts and forms

| Japanese | Kannada | Eke | Literally |
|----------|---------|-----|-----------|
| Karate | ಬರಿಗೈಕಾಳಗ | barigaykALaga | empty-hand fight |
| Kobujutsu | ಹಳೆಏರ್ಕಾಳಗ | haLeErkALaga | old weapon fight |
| Kata | ನಡೆಅಚ್ಚು | naDe accu | movement mould |

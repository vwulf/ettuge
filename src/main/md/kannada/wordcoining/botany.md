## Method notes

- **ಅಂಡ** looks Dravidian because of its retroflex, but it is Sanskrit *aṇḍa*. ಮೊಟ್ಟೆ (Tamil முட்டை) and ಕಾಳು are the native roots for egg and seed.
- The vetted set allows a little tadbhava Kannada, for example ದಳ. Words with retroflexes and no Sanskrit prefix are generally accepted in tadbhava form, and a few, such as ಮಣಿ, have uncertain etymologies and may be Dravidian.
- Coin independently, then vet against an accepted source, even one that isn't 100% Dravidian.

## Chair

ಕುರ್ಚಿ is usually listed as Persian (*kursī*), but the borrowing may have gone the other way. Kannada has a whole family of sitting verbs, ಕೂರು, ಕೂತು and ಕೂರಿಸು, and Persian has no verb root behind the noun. If that holds, ಕುರ್ಚಿ needs no replacement. The same thing happened with ಅಂಕಣ.

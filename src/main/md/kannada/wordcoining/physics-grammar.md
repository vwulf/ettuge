# Physics & Grammar Terms

*Newtonian mechanics, dimensions, and Bhat's tense–aspect–mood framework.*

---

## Motion and force

The root ಬೇಗ (speed, impetus) anchors the whole kinematic family.

| Concept | Kannada | Eke | Breakdown |
|---------|---------|-----|-----------|
| speed | ಬೇಗ | bEga | existing native word |
| linear momentum | ನಡೆಬೇಗ | naDebEga | ನಡೆ (move along) + ಬೇಗ |
| angular momentum | ತಿರುಗುಬೇಗ | tirugubEga | ತಿರುಗು (rotate) + ಬೇಗ |
| acceleration | ಬೇಗಏರಿಕೆ | bEgaErike | ಬೇಗ + ಏರಿಕೆ (rise) |
| mass | ಅಡಕ | aDaka | "packed content"; Bhat uses ಅಡಕ for a compressed form |
| force | ಒತ್ತಿಗೆ | ottige | ಒತ್ತು (press, exert) + -ಇಗೆ |

**F = ma** → **ಒತ್ತಿಗೆ = ಅಡಕ × ಬೇಗಏರಿಕೆ**

Rejected as Sanskrit: ಬಲ (*bala*), ಚಲನ (*calana*), ದ್ರವ್ಯರಾಶಿ. ತೂಕ is native, but it means weight, not mass.

## Dimensions — ಹರವು

ಹರವು (spread, extent) is the root, and the native numeral prefixes attach to it:

| English | Kannada | Eke |
|---------|---------|-----|
| 1D | ಒರ್ಹರವು | orharavu |
| 2D | ಇರ್ಹರವು | irharavu |
| 3D | ಮುರ್ಹರವು | murharavu |

> **Caveat:** ಬೇಗ may be a tadbhava of Sanskrit *vega*; I did not find it in Bhat, and the words built on it (ನಡೆಬೇಗ, ತಿರುಗುಬೇಗ, ಬೇಗಏರಿಕೆ) inherit that doubt. Bhat's ಹರವು (dimension) and ಪೊತ್ತು (tense) are attested; see the [Glossary](chat-glossary).

## Tense, aspect, mood

These follow Bhat's definitions in Book 32: tense is *when* an event happens, aspect is *how* it unfolds, and mood is the speaker's stance toward whether it is real or required.

| Category | Kannada | Eke | Breakdown |
|----------|---------|-----|-----------|
| tense | ಹೊತ್ತು | hottu | time, moment (ಕಾಲ is Sanskrit) |
| aspect | ಎಸಕನೋಟ | esakanOTa | ಎಸಕ (action) + ನೋಟ (view) |
| viewpoint aspect | ಎಸಕಒಳನೋಟ | esakaoLanOTa | adds ಒಳ (inner) |
| mood | ಒಳನಿಲುವು | oLaniluvu | ಒಳ (inner) + ನಿಲುವು (stance); ಮನ is Sanskrit |

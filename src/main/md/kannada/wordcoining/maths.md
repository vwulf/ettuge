# Maths — ಎ-ಎಣಿಕೆ

*A native notation for elementary functions, built around the single EML operator (eml(x, y) = eˣ − ln y).*

The vowels themselves carry the maths: ಏ → ಎ → ಇ goes from the full operator, to the derived one, to the imaginary.

## Symbols

| Symbol | Kannada | Eke | Meaning |
|--------|---------|-----|---------|
| ೦ | — | — | zero |
| ೧ | — | — | one |
| ಏ | — | E | exp, the full operator |
| ಅ | — | a | ln |
| ಎ | ಏ − ಅ | e | eml, the derived operator |
| ಇ | ಇಲ್ಲದ | illada | imaginary unit ("not there") |
| ತಿ | ತಿರುವು | tiruvu | angle θ ("a turn"); preferred over ಮೂಲೆ (corner) |
| ಗೊ | ಗೊತ್ತಿಲ್ಲದ | gottillada | unknown x |

## Trigonometry: the ಪಾಲು (ratio) family

| Function | Kannada | Eke | Short | Breakdown |
|----------|---------|-----|-------|-----------|
| sin | ನೇರಪಾಲು | nErapAlu | ನೇ | ನೇರ (directly opposite) + ಪಾಲು |
| cos | ಪಕ್ಕಪಾಲು | pakkapAlu | ಪ | ಪಕ್ಕ (adjacent) + ಪಾಲು |
| tan | ತಾಗುಪಾಲು | tAgupAlu | ತಾ | ತಾಗು (touch) + ಪಾಲು |
| π | ಸುತ್ತುಪಾಲು | suttupAlu | ಸು | ಸುತ್ತು (circumference) + ಪಾಲು |

ನೇರ was chosen over ಎದುರು for sin so that the ಎ- prefix stays reserved for the operator. For tan, ತಾಗುಪಾಲು ("touching ratio", the circle definition) beat ಓರೆಪಾಲು ("slope ratio", the triangle definition).

## Identities

| Identity | Native notation |
|----------|-----------------|
| sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 | ನೇ²ತಿ + ಪ²ತಿ = ೧ |
| Euler's formula | ಏ^(ಇ·ತಿ) = ಪ(ತಿ) + ಇ·ನೇ(ತಿ) |
| Euler's identity | ಏ^(ಇ·ಸು) + ೧ = ೦ |
| i² = −1 | ಇ² = −೧ |
| a quadratic | ಗೊ² + ೨ಗೊ + ೧ = ೦ |
